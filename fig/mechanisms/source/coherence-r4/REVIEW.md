# Paper figure review contract

Review figures in the compiled manuscript at their published size, not in
isolation at arbitrary zoom. For each revision collect the current render,
the previous version, and the relevant reference page or crop. The core
reference is Liu et al., *Selective Late Materialization in Modern Analytical
Databases*, PVLDB 18(11): Fig. 6/7 for workflow and mechanisms, Fig. 9 for
a compact multi-panel heatmap. Ask the strongest available visual reviewer
to inspect those images together and rank concrete changes. Avoid generic
comments. Before adopting a change, compare its actual reader task, not its
similarity to a reference.

Check both individual readability and figure-family consistency: serif fonts/
weights, circle and dash semantics, line rhythm, hue roles, and where shadows
encode focal objects. Use whitespace and alignment before adding boxes;
reserve heavy type and shallow shadows for one primary action per group.
Keep algorithm examples exact, and keep plot data and coordinates intact for
style-only iterations. Never introduce a second meaning for a mark just for
visual consistency. Render the final candidate again and compare it before
delivery. Record qualitative regressions and preserve the last accepted figure.
