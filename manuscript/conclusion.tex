\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We adapt external $k$-way merge to listwise ranking. SW keeps the rows it cannot yet emit and fills the resulting gaps from the input runs. LT($h$) keeps the losers in a tree and fetches the next segment along one path. The operator uses the same group mergers for materialized Full ranking and for \LIMIT~$K$, which pulls from its groups only as needed. The planner prices both calls and repeated row appearances when choosing the merge family, fan-in, and quota or segment length.
