Their graphics inform the mark weight, not the layout, phases, or algorithm. The inputs now share one tap directly into the existing run glyph. No box relocation, candidate changes, topology changes, fabricated scores, or extra execution link.

The token legend distinguishes fixed overhead from per-item tokens. “Materialize entire layer” versus “Push rows” / “Pull rows” keeps the Full boundary distinct from bidirectional per-group LIMIT traffic. Full/first K plans, stop condition and b(k)h ≤ m unchanged.
