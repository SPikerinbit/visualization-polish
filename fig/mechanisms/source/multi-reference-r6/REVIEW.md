# R5–R7 review checkpoints

Five independent specialist passes, then integrated review at manuscript size.

| Area | Primary material inspected as rendered art | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Framework | Liu et al., PVLDB 18(11), Figs 6–7; Shin et al., ListK, Fig. 1; Patel et al., LOTUS, Fig. 1 | Differentiate whole-layer barrier from group-level demand; preserve example semantics |
| Mechanisms | Liu et al. Figs 6–7; Kwon et al., vLLM, Fig. 6 | Show transitions without redundant containers; retain the verified numeric example and SW |
| 3D | Dao, FlashAttention-2, Fig. 5; Liu color-scale crop | Improve boundary and dash contrast and save legend space; keep original coordinates and the purple series |
| Lines | Kwon et al., vLLM, Fig. 12; Shin et al., ListK, Fig. 7 | Preserve existing ordering, shared legend and independent Full demand; no fitted trend for categorical points |
| Heatmap | Liu et al., Fig. 9; Meng et al., ROME, Fig. 2 | Keep the current shared diverging scale, cell grid and feasibility mask; no decorative additions |

Review checklist: use the same typography and marks when they mean the same
thing; prioritize actual reading order and direct labels over decoration.
Mechanism figures use rings for items and dash patterns for scope/state.
Experiment colors remain tied to quantitative or categorical variables.
Keep layout-review values separate from experimental claims.
